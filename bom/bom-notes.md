# BOM notes

- Costs are indicative USD prices for one prototype, estimated in September 2026 without supplier quotes. Every line is priced.
- Line numbers 1 to 12 and 17 match the exploded-view callouts in `media/exploded.png`. Lines 13 to 16 have no callout.
- Instrument total (lines 1 to 14 and 17): $335 against the $400 value-engineering target in `project.yaml` (a hypothetical control target; R13 within the target). Checked by `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py` [L1].
- Line 15, the rod extraction lever ($50), and line 16, the 500 mm extension rod with its coupling sleeve ($16), are optional and carried separately (CNP-DDR-001, D2; CNP-DDR-002, D10), so they are excluded from the instrument total and the R10 carried mass. With both the kit is $401, $1 over the value-engineering target; see the Value engineering section of the design decisions register (`docs/06-design-decisions.md`).
- TRL 3 changes: accelerometer and isolator added to the sensor pad (line 10, D1) with a band clamp instead of an anvil pocket (D3); bubble level on the handle (line 6, D1); magnets specified in the hammer (line 4); grooved aluminum drum, groove keeper and preloaded eye spring on the draw-wire reel (line 8, CNP-CAL-001 section E); optional lever (line 15, D2).
- DDR-002 changes (Amish, 2026-09-25): 6 mm plate, $28 to $24 (line 7); aluminum clamp and arm, $8 to $10 (line 9); 0.40 kg nylon roll bag instead of a 0.80 kg canvas bag, price unchanged (line 13); optional extension rod added (line 16). Instrument total $321 to $319; carried mass 16.7 kg to 15.7 kg.
- DDR-003 change (decided by Amish on 2026-09-27): the printed reel housing on line 8 grows from 60 x 56 x 60 mm to about 72 x 60 x 72 mm so the 60 mm drum fits with walls. The extra printed plastic is a few grams and a few cents, so the line price ($35) and the 0.30 kg reel mass estimate are unchanged. Stroke falls from 940 mm to 928 mm; the 850 mm usable range is unchanged.
- Decided on 2026-10-02 (CNP-DEC-001), not yet in `bom.csv`: rubber grips about 33 mm across on the T-handle (line 6); a screw-on end cap for the upper rod's M12 stud and a "fit the end cap" label for the hammer (line 14); no thread locker on the rod, anvil and cone joints (line 14 keeps it for the other fixings). Prices to be added at the next BOM revision.
- Not included: AA cells, the user's phone, and disposable cones for stiff ground (user guidance only, CNP-DDR-002, D12).
- Supplier types are given where no single supplier is known. Machined parts assume a local machine shop.
- DDR-004 changes (design for construction, 2026-09-30, made under Amish's instruction to make the design physically buildable; accepted by Amish on 2026-10-02 with one exception: no thread locker on the rod, anvil and cone joints): specs of lines 1 to 11, 14 and 16 now give the joints, fixings and sizes the build plan uses (M12 studs and tapped holes, magnet pockets, pinned stop collar and welded handle, one-piece clamp, reel internals and inserts, flanged logger box and glands, 20 mm coupling sleeve on the extension rod); line 17, the reel-to-logger cable, added ($6). Prices: line 6 $16 to $20 (welding), line 8 $35 to $38 (bearings, inserts, spring motor), line 14 $15 to $18 (screws, pin, inserts, epoxy), line 16 $15 to $16 (sleeve). Instrument total $319 to $335.

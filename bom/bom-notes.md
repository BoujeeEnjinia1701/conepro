# BOM notes

- Costs are indicative USD prices for one prototype, estimated in September 2026 without supplier quotes. Every line is priced.
- Line numbers 1 to 12 match the exploded-view callouts in `media/exploded.png`. Lines 13 to 15 have no callout.
- Instrument total (lines 1 to 14): $321 against the $400 budget in `project.yaml` (R13 met). Checked by `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py` [L1].
- Line 15, the rod extraction lever ($50), is optional and carried separately (CNP-DDR-001, D2), so it is excluded from the instrument total. With it the kit is $371, still within budget.
- TRL 3 changes: accelerometer and isolator added to the sensor pad (line 10, D1) with a band clamp instead of an anvil pocket (D3); bubble level on the handle (line 6, D1); magnets specified in the hammer (line 4); grooved aluminum drum, groove keeper and preloaded eye spring on the draw-wire reel (line 8, CNP-CAL-001 section E); optional lever (line 15, D2).
- Not included: AA cells, the user's phone, and an extension rod (the R5 1,000 mm clause needs one; proposed, awaiting Amish).
- Supplier types are given where no single supplier is known. Machined parts assume a local machine shop.
